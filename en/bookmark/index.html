SquadMeet on your phone

Set it up once. Then, on ome.tv, just tap “squad”.

This guide is for your phone.

Scan the code with your phone’s camera to open it there. On a computer it’s easier with the extension.

Do everything in Safari. It won’t work in Chrome or inside Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp.

You’re not in Safari right now. Copy this guide’s link, open Safari and paste it into the address bar.

Step 1 of 7

Copy the code

This is what makes SquadMeet show up on ome.tv. One tap copies it.

Step 2 of 7

Add this page to Favorites

  1. Tap Share. If you don’t see it, tap ⋯ and then Share.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Favorites.
  3. Tap Save. It’s already called “squad”: keep the name.
There’s no “Add to Favorites”

Swipe the list up: it’s usually further down. “Add Bookmark” works too if you pick the Favorites folder.

Step 3 of 7

Make the code its address

  1. Tap Bookmarks (or ⋯ → Bookmarks) and open Favorites.
  2. Tap Edit at the bottom, then tap squad.
  3. In the address, tap ⓧ to clear it, press and hold, tap Paste and then Done.
There’s no ⓧ

Tap once inside the address: the ⓧ shows up on the right. If it still doesn’t, press and hold inside, choose “Select All” and then “Paste”.

There’s no “Paste”

Go back to step 1 and tap “Copy code” again: the code stays on the clipboard until you copy something else.

Step 4 of 7

Try it right here

  1. Tap the address bar (at the bottom).
  2. Under Favorites, tap squad.
  3. If it’s set up right, this page notices on its own and moves to the next step.

It works! The bookmark is set up right.

A Google search opens

You tapped a search suggestion. Tap the bar and choose “squad” under Favorites, the icon with the “S”.

Nothing happens

The bookmark’s address isn’t right. Go back to step 3 and check that it starts with “javascript:”.

Step 5 of 7

Open ome.tv as the desktop site

  1. Tap Open ome.tv: Safari will take you to m.ome.tv.
  2. Tap aA (or ⋯) → Request Desktop Website. The button, not the settings. ome.tv will open.
  3. Now on ome.tv, tap aA → Website Settings → turn on Request Desktop Website. That way Safari remembers it.
Open ome.tv
It keeps bouncing between ome.tv and m.ome.tv

It happens if you turned it on in the settings while on m.ome.tv. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Request Desktop Website, tap Edit and delete m.ome.tv. Then repeat this step: first the button, then the settings, once you’re on ome.tv.

Step 6 of 7

Sign in with Facebook

  1. When ome.tv asks you to sign in, tap Facebook.
  2. With Google, Safari gets stuck loading: that’s ome.tv, not SquadMeet.
I don’t have Facebook

Open Settings → Apps → Safari and turn off “Prevent Cross-Site Tracking” and “Block Pop-ups”. Go back to ome.tv, reload and sign in with Google. You can turn “Block Pop-ups” back on afterwards.

Step 7 of 7

Done. Every time you want to use it

  1. Go to ome.tv.
  2. Tap the address bar and tap squad under Favorites.
  3. Tap Create group and send the link to your friends.
