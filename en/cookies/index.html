Cookie policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

We only use cookies if you accept Google Analytics. Everything else stored in your browser is there for the site to work and isn’t used to track you.

What they are

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for it to work; others are for measuring or advertising. SquadMeet has no advertising.
