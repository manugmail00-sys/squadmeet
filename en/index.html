How it works

Whoever has SquadMeet creates the group. Everyone else joins from their browser.

  1. Create the groupGo to ome.tv and press “Create group” in the SquadMeet panel.
  2. Send the linkOn WhatsApp or anywhere. Your friends open it on their phone or computer, nothing to install.
  3. Press StartThe next stranger sees all of you. Your friends see them big and hear the conversation.

Only whoever creates the group installs it. Pick where you’ll use Ome.tv.

Another way: automatic with the Userscripts app

The panel shows up on its own when you open ome.tv, but it takes longer to set up.

  1. Install Userscripts (free) and open it once.
  2. Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Userscripts → turn on and allow on “All Websites”.
  3. Open SquadMeet for Userscripts in Safari, tap aA (or ⋯) → Userscripts → Install.
  4. Put ome.tv in desktop mode once, as the guide explains.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Every day you get 10 free minutes with your group, and 30 more if you share SquadMeet on WhatsApp. To use it without limits there’s Pro: €5 once, forever, or €1.99 for 31 days. Only whoever creates the group pays.

How do I activate Pro?

By itself. On ome.tv, open the SquadMeet panel, tap “No limits with Pro”, type your email and pay on Payhip with that same email: when you’re done, Pro turns on in that browser with nothing else to do. If you bought it on the website or want it on another device, use the key you get by email (“I have a key”). It works on up to 3 devices.

Does the 31-day one renew by itself?

No. It isn’t a subscription: you pay €1.99 once and get Pro for 31 days. A few days before it ends, the panel reminds you in case you want to renew; if you buy another, 31 more days are added.

Do my friends need to install anything?

No. They open the link you send them, on their phone or computer, and allow the camera and microphone.

What does the stranger see?

A single picture with all of you, laid out on the screen, and they hear your voices mixed. Your friends see the stranger big with the group next to them.

Is anything recorded or stored?

No. Video and audio go directly between your browsers, encrypted. Our server only helps them find each other and keeps count of the free minutes. It’s all in the privacy policy.

My friend is stuck on “Joining…”

It’s usually the network: some mobile data networks block direct connections. Ask them to try Wi-Fi, or to close the page and open the link again.

Is there echo?

If someone has their speakers very loud, yes. It’s best if everyone uses headphones.

Can I get banned on Ome.tv?

It can happen. Ome.tv’s rules don’t allow add-ons that change how its website works, and SquadMeet swaps the video you send for the group’s. Use it at your own risk and always follow their rules.

Is SquadMeet part of Ome.tv?

No. SquadMeet is an independent project with no connection to Ome.tv.
