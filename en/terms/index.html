What SquadMeet is

SquadMeet lets whoever creates the group go on Ome.tv with their friends, combining their cameras into a single picture. It’s offered as a browser extension, bookmark and user script, together with this website.

Who can use it

Only people aged 18 or over, or the age of majority in your country if higher, same as Ome.tv.

Free and Pro

  • Free: 10 minutes a day with friends connected, plus 30 more minutes that day if you share SquadMeet on WhatsApp (once a day), with up to 3 friends. The time starts over every day.
  • Pro: no time limit, up to 5 friends, HD for your friends (if the connection allows) and group chat with “Next” voting. It’s paid once (€5, “forever”) or for 31 days (€1.99, no automatic renewal), taxes included.
  • SquadMeet sells Pro through Payhip, which handles payment (via Stripe or PayPal) and VAT. Pro goes with your SquadMeet account, which is the email you pay with: it turns on by itself when you pay and works in up to 3 browsers at a time (if you sign in on another, the one unused for longest is signed out). You also get a key by email. The account is personal: don’t share it.
  • The 31-day one isn’t a subscription: it doesn’t renew by itself and there’s nothing to cancel. It counts from the payment; if you buy another, 31 days are added to what you have left.
  • “Forever” means for as long as SquadMeet keeps running, with no further payments.
  • Refunds: if you’re not happy, write to contacto@squadmeet.app within 14 days of buying and we’ll refund you.

How to use it

  • Everyone in the group must agree to go on and to being seen and heard by the others.
  • Respect Ome.tv’s community rules and the people you talk to: no sexual content, harassment, insults, discrimination or anything illegal.
  • Don’t record, capture or share anyone without their permission.
  • Don’t try to get around the free limit or share your key, and don’t use SquadMeet’s servers for anything other than what the tool does. We may disable keys used that way.
